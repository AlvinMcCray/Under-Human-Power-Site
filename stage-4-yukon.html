Stage IV: Yukon River

AK, BC & YT • 2010

Overview

900 miles by canoe down the Yukon River from its headwaters near Whitehorse to the village of Ruby in interior Alaska. Following the historic route used by gold rush stampeders, this stage navigated one of North America's great waterways through remote subarctic wilderness.
