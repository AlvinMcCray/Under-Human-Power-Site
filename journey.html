The Journey

Six stages. 5,700 miles. Seven years. One expedition under human power.

From the sun-scorched Mexican border to the frozen Arctic Ocean, Darrell J. Gardner's expedition spanned the length of western North America entirely under human power. The journey unfolded across six distinct stages, each presenting its own challenges, landscapes, and stories.
